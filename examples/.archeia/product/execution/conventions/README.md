# Conventions

**Canonical meaning.** The living statement of local defaults, preferences, and standard ways of doing things within a domain or subdomain.

**Broad interpretation.** May capture naming conventions, authoring defaults, expected review patterns, default sequencing, recurring heuristics, and house style. `conventions/` is softer than formal external standards and stronger than individual preference. Scoped to the `product/execution` subtree.

Answer here (even "N/A" or "not applicable yet"):

- What local defaults or ways of working apply here?
- What would we want every contributor to do the same way in this subtree?

**What belongs here:**
- Local conventions records scoped to this subtree.

**What does not belong here:**
- Unrelated project knowledge, hidden wrapper folders, or content owned by another top-level domain.
