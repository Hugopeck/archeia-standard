# Decisions

**Canonical meaning.** The accumulating record of local choices, tradeoffs, and rulings within a domain or subdomain.

**Broad interpretation.** May capture ADR-style technical decisions, strategic decisions, design rulings, operating policy choices, or growth-program decisions as long as the decision remains local to the owning path. Scoped to the `product/execution` subtree.

Answer here (even "N/A" or "not applicable yet"):

- What choices or tradeoffs belong in this local decisions record?
- What context would a future agent need to understand why this decision was made?

**What belongs here:**
- Local decisions records scoped to this subtree.

**What does not belong here:**
- Unrelated project knowledge, hidden wrapper folders, or content owned by another top-level domain.
